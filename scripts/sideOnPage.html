Croquet side-on strokes

Chosen shots replayed from the engine, at the grip the push map finds, on the 10 s lawn. All panels share one clock, with 0 ms at contact. Prototype: fixed shots, no live inputs.

Striker's ball Croqueted ball Leading face and its path Mallet, hands on the shaft Top hand's path

Balls move as simulated through the impact. After it they carry on from their handover speed under gravity, ignoring the turf, so the departure shows direction and pace only; the distances quoted are the engine's full roll-out. Hands are drawn fixed on the shaft, so a slip is reported as text and not drawn. The engine stops following the mallet at its finish, so the mallet then holds still on the page.
