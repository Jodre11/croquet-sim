# P2b.2b.2c.3b.1 shapes Implementation Plan

Executed natively in the main session, task by task; the gates stop for the user.

**Goal:** One finish for every stroke, the half roll as a drive with lean, a diagnosis of the rolls' lean runaway, and
side-on traces of the four strokes for the user to judge.

**Architecture:**
- The push's eased stop is replaced by a hands' point pendulum on their arc from the push's end (track.ts `Push`).
- From a release time, a second free-pendulum table drives the mallet's pitch (track.ts `PreparedTrack.finish`).
- integrate.ts loses the "carry" finish kind, and a swing ends at the mallet's apex after the release.
- The half roll's presets become the drive's.
- pathTrace.ts gains diagnosis columns and the four-stroke check.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, tsx scripts.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-p2b2b2c3b1-shapes-design.md (parent:
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-p2b2b2c3b1-level-head-design.md §11).

## Global Constraints

- ENGINE_VERSION "0.20.0" (src/engine/simulate.ts).
- The finish at zero push with λ = 0 is bit-identical to today's (a drive, single-ball and both stops without a push).
- HAND_COUPLING, ARM_FLEX and ratioFit are not changed.
- No envelope, push-map or ratioFit runs. Long scripts run under `nice -n 20`.
- 4 spaces, 120 columns, UK English. Doc comments on exported APIs cite the spec section ("shapes design §2").
- Every re-pinned test value carries a one-line reason in its comment.

## Review Focus

1. A fully held roll whose pitch rate is already 0 at the push's end must not finish at its first step (Task 2 test).
2. A push whose end is never found (push.end null) has no release: it runs to FOLLOW_CAP and must not throw (Task 2
   test).
3. A no-push stroke with `stroke.slopeHold` > 0 releases at its held path's rest, not at contact (Task 2 test).
4. A fast push whose hands would pass a quarter turn fails with the named error, not NaN (Task 1 test).
5. Practice (practice.ts) reads `prepareTrack(…, false).push`. The new fields must not need the finish table, so it
   still runs with `withFree` false (Task 2 test: practice.test.ts passes unchanged apart from re-pins).

### Task 5: The fix (amended after gate 1)

Defined at gate 1. Its acceptance targets are fixed now: the played lean within 5° of the plan through the push, and
the top hand within 10 mm of its path, on the full and pass roll text books. Neither HAND_COUPLING nor the bottom hand's
path may move.

**Amendment (gate 1, user 2026-11-01: "Agree").** The diagnosis and its check (the probe's addendum) put the
runaway on the balls' moment about the top hand, not on their force. Without the balls both rolls follow their plan, and
meeting the resistance along aim at once does not help. So the fix is the hands holding the shaft's pitch through the
push, as a stop's hold does:
- In `carryLoad`, while a push runs (t ≤ push.end, or always if its end is never found), add the couple
  M_p·n about the pitch axis n. M_p = K_p·e + C_p·ė, with:
  - e the pitch error, from the path's orientation to the head's;
  - ė the pitch rate's lag;
  - K_p and C_p from `holdPitchGains(track.coupling, I_P)`, where I_P = I_y + M·a² is the swung body's pitch inertia
    about the top hand.

  M_p is held to ±`gripCouple(hands, arc.radius)`. Nothing else changes.
- Test (track or hands tests): a pushed stroke's hand torque carries the couple and it is bounded by gripCouple; a
  stroke without a push carries none.
- Run the pathTrace diagnosis on both text books, practised and unpractised, and append it to the probe, against the
  targets above. Re-pin what moved, with reasons, and bump ENGINE_VERSION. Commit: "Hold the shaft's pitch through the
  push".

**Amendment (user 2026-11-01: "I think that is realistic").** With the hold the lean meets its target, but the balls
still push the hands 35–50 mm back and 45–75 mm up off their path while they are on the face. The user accepts that
give as realistic, so the top hand's 10 mm target is dropped and its offset is read on the traces, not gated. Two
changes ride with the hold:
- the stop's hold and the push's share one clamp, `heldPitch(coupling, inertia, error, lag, bound)`, tested on its own;
- the finish's watch (integrate.ts `finished`) counts a rise only while the pitch rate gains. A rate the hold carried
  positive through the release, and dying away within 3 ms, ended the unpractised full roll at 104 ms, not at its
  mallet's apex (978 ms).
