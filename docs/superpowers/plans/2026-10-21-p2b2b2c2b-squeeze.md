# P2b.2b.2c.2b — The Croquet Stroke's Squeeze: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A croquet stroke can be set up with the balls 0 to < 2 mm apart (default 1 mm); the strike is judged
against Gugan 4's ten filmed strokes and is step-converged; a bounded search looks for what keeps truly touching
balls apart; Gugan 5's drive and stop, the scatter and the other checks are recorded and ruled on at the gate.

**Architecture:**

- `src/engine/shot.ts`: `validateSetup` accepts a croquet stroke's croqueted ball 0 to < 2 mm from the striker's
  (`CROQUET_GAP_LIMIT`). The engine's only change, unless the convergence fix (Task 5) or an admitted candidate
  (Task 7) adds one.
- `tests/engine/support/shot.ts`: `canonicalSetup` gains `croquetGap`, its default `DEFAULT_CROQUET_GAP` (0 until
  Task 9, then 1 mm).
- `scripts/filmedStrokes.ts` (new): Gugan 4's Tables 1, 2 and 5 and Fig. 4 as sourced figures.
- `scripts/ratioFit.ts`: the impact step as an option, the read-out (spec §5.2), Gugan's effective mass (the solved
  head mass), and Gugan's setups.
- `scripts/squeeze.ts` (new): the free-collision harness (spec §4.1 test 4) and the search's candidate runs.
- `scripts/croquetFitProbe.ts`: new sections D–G and I–M (spec §9); section A re-run as the distance check.
- The tasks land in the spec's ratchet order (§8.2): the baseline and the tidy; the gap at default 0; the sources;
  the read-out; the step convergence; the measurements; the search; the checks; the default gap; the gate; the
  documents; the version.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (strict, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`, `verbatimModuleSyntax`), Vitest, ESLint, Prettier. No
new dependencies. `npx --yes tsx` runs the scripts.

**Decisions made while planning.** Task 11 folds each into the spec's Amendments.

1. **The engine's check.** `export const CROQUET_GAP_LIMIT = 0.002` in `src/engine/shot.ts` (comment: the user's
   "< 2mm" and the Laws' Commentaries' 2 mm for nominally touching, spec "My calls"). The check becomes
   `if (!(gap < CROQUET_GAP_LIMIT))`, message `croqueted ball ${croqueted} must lie within 2 mm of the striker (gap
   ${gap} m)`. A negative gap (overlap) stays as today: `validateImpact`'s overlap check owns it.
2. **The set-up option.** `CanonicalOptions.croquetGap?: number` (m, surface to surface), for a croquet stroke only;
   `export const DEFAULT_CROQUET_GAP` beside `GC_STOP_GAP`. `canonicalSetup` throws an `Error` prefixed
   `canonicalSetup:` for `croquetGap` outside [0, CROQUET_GAP_LIMIT), for `croquetGap` on a non-croquet stroke, and
   (as today) for `targetGap` on a croquet stroke. The range is checked on the input, not on the placed positions,
   so round-off in 2R + g never decides it. The croqueted ball's centre lies 2R + g along the stroke's line (the pass
   roll's 20° kept).
3. **The impact step.** `strike` and `runFit` take a last optional `options: { readonly dt?: number } = {}`, passed to
   `simulateImpact` as its `dt`. Absent, the call is today's to the bit.
4. **The read-out** (spec §5.2, "My calls"). `STRIKE_WINDOW = 5e-3` s and `READ_DELAY = 1e-3` s. `readTimeOf` counts
   only face and ball–ball intervals that begin within STRIKE_WINDOW of the first face interval's start; the read time
   is READ_DELAY after the latest end among them; `open` is true when a counted face interval has not ended by
   STRIKE_WINDOW. U and W are the head centre's velocity along aim (`dot(v, aim)`), U at the first face interval's
   start, W at the read time; w₁ and v₂ are each ball's horizontal speed at the read time. If the impact ends before
   the read time, the figures are its last step's, and the reported time is that step's.
5. **Gugan's mallet.** `GUGAN_EFFECTIVE_MASS = 2.3` (ball masses; Gugan 5, "by taking moments about its point of
   balance"). `gugansMallet` bisects `headMass` over [0.5, 2] × the default's, 60 halvings, until the swung body's
   effective mass at the face centre along aim (`massOf`'s arithmetic, `src/engine/impact/track.ts:205`, `:250`) on
   the preset's canonical setup at the stroke's speed equals 2.30 · m_b. `canonicalSetup`'s `mallet` option already
   re-leans the stance to the default lean (`tests/engine/support/shot.ts:240-246`); the solve checks that lean, it
   does not undo it.
6. **The free-collision harness.** `freeCollision` plays the impact's ball–ball law alone through
   `integrate(isolated(…))` (`tests/engine/support/impact.ts:305`), as `tests/engine/impact/analytic.test.ts:55`
   does: two balls, one moving, no head, no turf, no gravity. The video's time runs from the struck ball's displacement
   first exceeding `VISIBLE_SHIFT` to the pair's gap first exceeding it. Task 3 sets `VISIBLE_SHIFT` from Gugan 4's
   stated resolution if it gives one, else 0.1 mm, and records which in the README.
7. **Candidates b and e change a law.** They run in a throwaway copy of the worktree (`<temp>/candidates/<b|e>/`),
   never committed: only their figures reach the probe file. A candidate the gate admits lands later as its own task.
8. **Order.** The filmed strokes' machinery (Task 6) comes before the search (Task 7), because the search's test 3 is
   §5's gapped strokes. The search's gate section B is put to the user at once if a candidate passes (spec §4.3, "the
   default moves last … so that a candidate the gate admits can re-open it").
9. **The probe's sections.** A (kept: the distance check, §7.4), B, C and H (kept, dormant or as they are); new: D
   gap 0 re-measured and the gap sweep (§9), E the filmed strokes (§5), F the timing (§7.1), G the convergence (§6),
   I the search (§4), J Gugan 5's drive and stop across the gap (§7.2), K the scatter (§7.3), L the share (§7.8), M
   the jab, the AC stop's re-contacts and the effective mass (§7.5–§7.7). `all` runs A, D–G, I–M in that order; B, C
   and H only by name.

## Review Focus

The spec implies each input below, but none of its listed tests exercises it. The owning task pins each with a test.

1. **A gap at the limit, through round-off.** `croquetGap: 0.002` must throw in `canonicalSetup` whatever 2R + g
   rounds to, and the engine must reject a hand-built setup whose computed gap is 0.002 + 1e-12 m and accept one at
   0.002 − 1e-9 m. Pinned in Task 2 ("decides the limit on the input, not the rounded positions").
2. **Balls overlapping by round-off.** A croqueted ball 5e-10 m inside the striker's (gap −5e-10) is accepted by
   `validateSetup` as today; only `validateImpact` judges overlap. Pinned in Task 2 ("still accepts a croqueted ball
   overlapping within CONTACT_TOLERANCE").
3. **A gap where none belongs.** `croquetGap` on a single-ball stroke, and `targetGap` on a croquet stroke, both throw
   with the option's name. Pinned in Task 2 ("rejects a gap option on the wrong kind of stroke").
4. **A strike that has not finished, or an impact that ends early.** A face interval still open at STRIKE_WINDOW sets
   `open`; an impact ending before the read time reads its last step and reports that time; neither yields NaN or a
   missing figure. Pinned in Task 4 ("flags a strike still open at the window's end" and "reads an impact's last step
   when it ends before the read time").
5. **An effective mass out of reach.** If 2.30 · m_b lies outside the bracket's effective masses, `gugansMallet`
   throws a `RangeError` naming the preset and both ends, never returning an end. Pinned in Task 4 ("throws when the
   target mass lies outside the bracket").
