== The bound's cause (B-d7), from scratch scripts on 2b5df59, deleted after

The stroke has two stages, and the ratio is set by whether and how the second happens.

1. The first strike is the same in every play. Traced from first face contact on both rolls, held slack or firm, with
   a push or none: by +8 ms the head has fallen from about 2.96 to 1.35 m/s, the striker's ball is at about 1.4 m/s and
   the croqueted ball at about 2.65 m/s. Those speeds are the no-push stroke's: ratio 2.02 to 2.18, range about 1.4 m.
   The striker's ball goes down into the turf and comes back, z 0.0425 m at +4 ms to 0.0502 m at +20 ms (rest 0.0456 m).
2. The second stage is the head catching the ball again, with the push behind it. It carries most of the range. Where
   it happens, the face mostly touches for 100 to 300 ms in 5 to 27 separate contacts, the range is about 2.4 to 9 m
   and the ratio about 0.6 to 1.6. Where it does not, the stroke keeps the first strike's ratio.

Cause 1, nearly all of the bound: the found hold's rule picks a stroke with no second stage. At sigma 0 the head rides
up over the ball. Pass roll, drive 0.5, handReach 0.3 m, window 20 ms: head height 0.144 m to 0.217 m and lean 32 deg to
44.4 deg by +120 ms, the last face contact by about +20 ms, striker's ball at 1.2 m/s. Nothing fails, since there is
nothing left to hit and the lean stays under 45 deg, so it is admissible and the search takes it as the least hold. The
same plan held harder plays the second stage. At the same power the two differ by about 4 times in range:

hardly moves: median spread over the admissible holds 0.033 on the full roll, 0.026 on the pass roll.
