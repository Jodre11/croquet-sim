Viewer full range: smoke test (hands-from-lean plan Task 3; design §5)
2026-11-12, branch viewer-full-range, Vite dev server, the app's built-in browser pane.

1. Each label's default at its range (pass roll, full roll, half roll, drive, AC stop, single ball)
   Every default loads with "Envelope: viable"; the extras' sliders read the type's stance and shape
   (drive: grips 1.00 / 0.25, follow-through 200 ms; half roll: 1.00 / 1.00).

2. Each of the eight sliders at each end, per label (16 moves a label, 96 in all)
   - Every move replays: the Run is discarded and the swing plays from the planned path's first sample.
   - "not swept for <type>" shows at every end beyond the type's graded span or with an extra moved:
     lean −12.0° and 45.0°, feet −374 and +431 mm, window 1 and 100 ms, either grip at 0.05, follow-through
     0 and 300 ms (and the drive's bottom grip at 1.00, its default being 0.25). Push length and after contact
     at their ends stay on the grid where the type's table grades them.
   - Run is disabled, with the planner's reason, only at lean −12.0°: "the head is in the turf at contact" on the
     rolls and the AC stop, "no power at this range" on the drive and single ball; and on the half roll with a grip
     at 0.05: "the grip cannot hold the planned slope (slopeHold 1)".

3. Timings (the input handler's synchronous work: plan, judge, planned path)
   - Pane shown: 25–42 ms a move across the six labels; plannedPath alone 53–63 ms (five-call mean, each type's
     text book at power 1).
   - Pane hidden (a background page, throttled): 45–350 ms a move; planStroke alone 184–286 ms on both the drive
     and the pass roll, so the rise is the throttling, not the type. The spec's "under 100 ms" is met with the pane
     shown; the hidden figures are recorded but not judged.

4. A Run, then a slider move (half roll default)
   - Run: the found hold searched and played in 4.6 s, "Run: admissible".
   - Feet moved 10 mm after the Run: the Run badge cleared, the found hold back to "searched on Run", replayed.
   - Run again, feet moved back during its search: the result arrived 8 s later and was dropped (no Run badge; the
     shown swing stays the inputs' planned one).
   - An off-grid set-up (drive, lean 34.4°, bottom grip 0.50) Run: found hold 0.0543, "Run: admissible".

5. The plumb line and foot marker (design §5.5)
   Drive default at 4.59 m: the plumb from the top hand labelled "hands −98 mm", the foot marker "feet −186 mm",
   the feet slider's value; the marker stays at the reading through the swing, the plumb follows the top hand.
   Each type's text-book feet equals feetReading's lowPoint to 1e-6 m (screen.test.ts).
