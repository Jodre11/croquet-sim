Conclusion

The evidence points to the gap-0 contact chain itself: the overlapping face law and ball-ball law acting together. The
hands, the turf, gravity, the shaft and friction are not the source. The evidence for this is strong:

- the bare 1-D chain on the engine's own two laws reproduces the gap-0 excess and its fall with gap (1.21 at gap 0, 1.15
  at 0.25 mm, 1.08 at 1 mm, on both sets of laws);
- at the model's effective mass the bare chain sits within 0.02 of the full stroke (1.278 against 1.257; 1.251 against
  1.230);
- the full stroke's extra ball-ball impulse at gap 0 (+0.13 to +0.14 m.U over 1 mm) arrives entirely within the single,
  overlapping first face contact;
- decoupling the hands changes v2/U by under 0.003.

The mechanism: at gap 0 the face's Hertz contact and the linear ball-ball contact are loaded at the same time. Their
time scales are close (T_bb 0.55 ms against T_face 0.51-0.65 ms; today's 0.75 against 0.78-1.0), so the three-body
compression releases with the head's momentum passed forward into ball 2. This transfer exceeds both rigid 1-D limits
(sequential 1.10-1.12, lumped 1.00-1.02) and dissipates less than the sequential collisions do. Scaling both laws' times
together (today's against admitted) leaves it unchanged, which is why no re-set of t0 alone moves it.

The model's effective mass, 2.51 ball masses against Gugan's 2.30, adds a minor +0.04. Even at 2.30 the chain gives
1.235 and 1.208.

What could not be determined:

- whether the real touching pair's contact (Hertzian and non-linear, with its own rate-dependent loss) would give the
  same matched transfer. The engine's ball-ball law is linear; no Hertzian ball-ball variant was built, which would need
  an engine edit;
- whether the face law's k and c, set from the closing speed on a single ball's reduced mass, are right when the face
  drives two touching balls. Swapping the reduced mass between the bare head and M_eff moved v2/U by 0.003; a reduced
  mass on 2m was not tried;
- how far the result hangs on the restitution the ball-ball law has in a chain. Its e is fixed for a free pair, yet in
  the chain the linear dashpot keeps more energy;
- whether Gugan's gap-0 strokes were truly touching (he does not mark C3S and C20S nominal).
